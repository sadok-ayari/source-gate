## The claim under scrutiny

The programme has restored 12,400 hectares of degraded land across three regions since 2023 [S1].

Financing is committed at USD 40 million over five years, with the first disbursement tied to a verified hectare count [S2].

Seasonal jobs created to date total 620, of which 38 per cent are held by women [S1, S4].

## Where this brief is thin

Implementation is on track and the client is satisfied.

The programme is the most ambitious of its kind in the region.
